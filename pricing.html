Common questions

Before you sign up

Do I need my own racket?

No. We have rackets to borrow for your first sessions, and the coaches will tell you what to look for before you buy one.

What if I have never played?

Come to a trial session. You get put in a group at your level, not thrown in with people who have played for years.

What are the points for?

You earn them by turning up, arriving on time and doing your home drills, then spend them at the kantine on drinks, grips and club gear.

Can I pause a cycle?

Yes, if you tell us before the cycle starts. Missed sessions inside a running cycle can be made up with another group when there is space.

Come and hit some balls

Tell us your level and when you can train, and we will put you in a group for a trial session.
